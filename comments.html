🎓 HOW STORED XSS WORKS

Unlike Reflected XSS (which only affects the person who clicks a malicious link), Stored XSS saves the payload into the database. Every visitor who loads this page will execute your script — automatically, with no link-clicking required.

The comment form sends your input to comment.php, which inserts it directly into the database with no sanitization. The page then renders it back with no escaping.
